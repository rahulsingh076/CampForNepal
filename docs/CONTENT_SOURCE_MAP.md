## Required Source Review Before Public Launch

Use the official Department of Immigration, Nepal Tourism Board, protected-area
authority, airline, and insurer sources for any visa, permit, fee, weather,
flight, insurance, or closure claim. Record the source URL, verification date,
next review date, and owner approval before replacing the current demo copy.
